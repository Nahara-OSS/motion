## Some notes

This section describe what to _expect_ from Nahara's Motion. Skip to
[this section](#introduction) if you just want to get started right away.

Nahara's Motion Editor is **not meant to be a drop-in replacement** for your
favorite motion graphics app (if you've used one before). There are some parts
from your favorite app that are missing in Nahara's Motion (or maybe there is
but with different way of doing things), and there are some parts that's much
faster to do in Nahara's Motion than it is to do in other apps. As such, it is
preferred to approach Nahara's Motion Editor with the mindset of _"What can I do
with Nahara's Motion?"_ instead of _"Can I do what I've been doing in X?"_. See
[this page](../bonus/not-a-replacement.md) to learn more.
