## Introduction

Welcome to Nahara's Motion user manual!

Nahara's Motion is an editor and motion graphics engine for motion graphics,
powered by GPU acceleration and unique scene architecture. All of Nahara's
Motion components are licensed under free software licenses, and Nahara's Motion
Editor is distributed free of charge by Nahara's Organization.

- **Free of charge**: You can obtain a copy of Nahara's Motion Editor for free
  from the official download website.

- **Free software licenses**: Nahara's Motion Editor is licensed under GPLv3,
  granting you the freedom with this software and ensuring your freedom stays
  intact. Additionally, Nahara's Motion Engine is licensed under MIT license,
  allowing you to integrate the engine in your own programs.

## Where to start?

Most people open this user manual expects to learn how to get started with the
editor, and so the [Editor quick start guide](./editor/quick-start.md) is the
perfect place to get used to Nahara's Motion Editor. We highly recommend browing
the entire manual though, as it contains the documentation for every little
things that could positively impact your workflow.

If you choose to explore Nahara's Motion in-depth right away, we recommend
[the full introduction to the editor](./editor/index.md), which is much longer
than quick start guide, but also more detailed and recommended for those that
willing to use Nahara's Motion exclusively.

Some people might prefer to learn from doing the samples, and so we also have
[the tutorials](./tutorials/index.md) that you can take a look at and follow
along.
