02

Simply powerful

Simple tree-based scene system with powerful features.

  • Animate with keyframes or expand your brain with math expressions
  • Make multiple copies with Duplicate object
  • Advanced text animation with Selector and Transform modifiers
  • Puppet animation with 2D Meshes and modifiers
  • Music visualization with Analyze as keyframe and Spectrum
  • Extend your tool with plugins and more!
